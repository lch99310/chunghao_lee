# Image Prompts

## `pic.png` — Opening scene / thumbnail

Placement: after the opening morning-commute scene.

Purpose: let the reader immediately feel what frictionless personal AI interaction looks like before the article explains the architecture.

```text
Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: editorial article hero and thumbnail
Primary request: Create a wide editorial conceptual illustration that depicts one ordinary morning in a near-future personal AI experience.
Scene/backdrop: An understated urban morning near a train station. A calm professional is walking toward the station with a work bag, wearing small wireless earbuds and subtle, normal-looking smart glasses. The phone remains clearly inside a coat or trouser pocket; the person is not holding or looking at it.
Subject/action: The person is speaking naturally while walking, asking AI about a previous project decision. Show a restrained amber voice signal traveling from the earbud toward the phone in the pocket. Around the phone, use a faint editorial cutaway or translucent memory collage suggesting meeting notes, a past comparison table, and one recorded concern being retrieved. A soft response returns to the earbud. In the smart-glasses field of view, show only a tiny, clean checklist of three abstract lines with check marks, without readable words.
Narrative sequence: One continuous visual moment should imply question → memory retrieval → cloud reasoning → spoken answer → short visual checklist, while keeping the scene human and believable rather than turning it into a technical flowchart.
Style/medium: New York Times op-ed / The Economist editorial illustration; clean flat shapes, thin charcoal linework, restrained conceptual storytelling, subtle paper grain.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape. The walking person occupies the central-left area; the station entrance sits ahead on the right. Keep generous negative space and a clear visual path from earbud to pocket phone and back. The person's forward movement must remain readable.
Lighting/mood: Quiet early-morning light, thoughtful and optimistic, familiar rather than futuristic.
Color palette: Warm off-white paper background #f6f3ec, muted slate and soft grey, near-black charcoal outlines, a single warm amber accent #b3701f for the invisible AI interaction.
Text: No written headline, labels, logos, brand names, or readable interface text inside the image.
Constraints: The phone must stay in the pocket. The person must not touch or look at the phone. Earbuds and smart glasses should look mature and unobtrusive, not like science-fiction props. The image must match the article's opening scene rather than explain the entire system architecture.
Avoid: glossy 3D rendering, photorealistic stock-photo look, neon, holographic dashboards, cyberpunk city, robots, floating brains, dense circuitry, app icons, oversized AR overlays, garbled text, logos, dystopian mood.
```

## `pic2.png` — Personal AI system infographic

Placement: after "The Cloud Thinks. The Phone Remembers. // 雲端是大腦，手機是記憶".

Purpose: make the article's complete argument understandable at a glance: AI reasoning lives in the cloud, while the personal memory that makes the answer relevant lives on the phone.

```text
Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: inline editorial systems infographic
Primary request: Create a clean editorial infographic showing a distributed personal AI system built from devices that already exist.
Scene/backdrop: Warm off-white editorial paper with subtle grain, no framed dashboard and no dark software-flowchart background.
Central subject: A large upright smartphone at the center. The phone screen must not show app icons. Instead, show a clearly organized "Personal LLM Wiki" as a layered personal memory library made of small document pages, photos, voice-waveform cards, video frames, calendar items and decision notes. The phone should visibly function as memory and control, not as the AI brain.
Left: A human figure wearing wireless earbuds, speaking naturally toward the phone. A clear directional arrow carries the conversation from the person to the phone.
Top: A cloud AI inference node, visually distinct from the phone and without a company logo. An upward arrow carries only selected memory/context from the phone to the cloud. A return arrow carries the personalized answer back toward the phone.
Right: A pair of normal-looking smart glasses showing a concise visual summary or three-line checklist. A branch from the personalized answer flows toward both the earbuds and the glasses.
Bottom: One prominent continuous loop connects the full interaction cycle. Use these exact short labels in order: "CONVERSATION" → "RETRIEVE MEMORY" → "CLOUD INFERENCE" → "ANSWER" → "UPDATE MEMORY". The final arrow must visibly return into the Personal LLM Wiki inside the phone.
Core visual hierarchy: The phone is the largest and central object. The cloud is above it. The person is left. The glasses are right. The write-back loop closes at the bottom. The reader should understand the system before reading any label.
Style/medium: New York Times graphics desk / Economist editorial infographic; precise flat illustration, thin charcoal linework, calm information design, no decorative effects.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape with generous whitespace. Balanced radial composition around the central phone. Use arrows sparingly but make every direction unambiguous.
Typography: Minimal editorial typography. Small uppercase sans-serif labels for the five loop stages. Use the exact phrase "PERSONAL LLM WIKI" once inside the phone. All text must be correctly spelled and highly legible.
Color palette: Warm paper #f6f3ec, charcoal #242424, muted slate for devices, warm amber #b3701f reserved for active arrows, retrieved memory and the write-back loop.
Conceptual message: The cloud is where AI reasons; the phone is where the memory that makes AI understand the person lives.
Constraints: Clearly separate cloud reasoning from phone memory. Show both answer delivery and memory write-back. The phone must not resemble a generic home screen. The glasses should display only a short summary, not a full interface.
Avoid: conventional box-and-arrow software diagram, dark background, glossy 3D, gradients, neon, glowing brain, humanoid robot, network-map clutter, floating app icons, company logos, decorative data streams, illegible or garbled text.
```
