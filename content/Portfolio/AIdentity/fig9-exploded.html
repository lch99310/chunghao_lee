Nine parts. Every one is in production today.

What is inside AIdentity, and what was deliberately left out.
NO.PART · WHAT IT DOES
1Top cap · zirconia ceramic
Turns 90° on a magnetic detent to go private. Lets radio and light through.
2Light guide + LEDs
The seam of light: off, soft white, blue pulse, amber.
3Hall sensor + detent magnets
Reads the cap's position. No button, no app toggle.
4Logic board
Wearable-class SoC with NPU · 5G RedCap modem + eSIM · Wi-Fi 7 / Bluetooth LE Audio · UWB · 1 TB storage · secure element for keys.
5Thermal spreader · graphite + copper
Carries heat into the aluminium shell.
6Battery · ≈ 10 Wh pouch cell
About three days at an average 0.13 W (estimate).
7Body shell · anodized aluminium
Two 0.6 mm antenna breaks on opposite vertical corners.
8Qi2 coil + magnet ring
Snaps onto the dock. Charging is when memory is consolidated.
9Base plate · zirconia ceramic
Transparent to the charging field.
Left out on purpose: camera · microphone · speaker · display · a large model on the device. Each would add heat, cost or other people's suspicion, and your glasses, earbuds and cloud models already do those jobs.
