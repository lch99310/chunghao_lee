Fig. 7 · Trust without a screen

Show the state, not the contents.

One line of light, one twist, and a weekly statement, the way a bank earns trust without showing you the vault.
The line of light: four states, nothing else
Off
Idle. Nothing is coming in or going out.
Soft white
Receiving from your glasses, earbuds or band.
Blue pulse
Context is going to a cloud model, for this task only.
Amber
Private. Top turned; no device feeds memory.
The quarter turn
Seen from above. A quarter turn of the ceramic cap pauses memory intake from every connected device at once. The cap stays flush, but its notch moves to the side, so the state is visible to you and to anyone at the table.
The weekly statement
Read aloud or shown in your glasses on Sunday. Like a bank statement: totals you can check, and any line you can question.
